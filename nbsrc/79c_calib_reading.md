**Reading.** The released models *are* over-confident on natural data (the best temperature is 1.1 to 1.75, i.e. their distributions are too sharp), but a temperature recovers only a minority of what averaging seeds buys: about 40% on text and images, 10 to 25% on speech and DNA. Most of the ensemble gain is real: different seeds get different things right. Even so, on text a 3-seed ensemble (5.40 bits) is still worse than the untrained counter (4.76). *(The temperature is one scalar per model fitted on natural data; it is used only for this diagnosis, never for any number reported as a result.)*
